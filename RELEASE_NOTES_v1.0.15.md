IRICA v1.0.15 — Softer dark theme

What's new
----------
- The dark theme is noticeably lighter and easier on the eyes: the
  near-black backgrounds are replaced with a soft graphite-indigo palette
  across the launcher card, the customs panel (dark mode), the inquiry
  panel (dark mode), and the home picker. Text contrast and shadows were
  tuned to match.

Upgrade
-------
Double-click IRICA-Setup-v1.0.15.exe. Your data in %LOCALAPPDATA%\irica
(profiles, settings, logs) is untouched. If the panel is running, the
installer closes it first. Close any manually-started panel yourself.

SmartScreen note
----------------
The installer is not code-signed. If Windows SmartScreen appears:
click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
